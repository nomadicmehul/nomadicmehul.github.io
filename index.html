About Me

Building the Future of Cloud Technology

A passionate tech enthusiast from Berlin, Germany, with a Master's in Computer Science and a mission to democratize DevOps knowledge.

I'm Mehul Patel, a DevOps and Cloud expert with over 12 years of hands-on experience in cutting-edge technologies like AWS, Google Cloud, Docker, Kubernetes, Ansible, Jenkins, and Terraform. Sharing knowledge is my driving force.

Through my initiative CloudCaptain, I've conducted over 500 sessions across 25+ countries, helping individuals and organizations excel in DevOps and cloud technologies. I'm honored to serve in leadership roles, including the Mozilla Reps Council, Auth0 by Okta Ambassador, and founder of OSW and OSCF.

I actively organize events and build communities at CNCF Gandhinagar, HashiCorp Gandhinagar, and GDG Cloud Gandhinagar. Currently based in Berlin, I'm focused on AI-powered DevOps, self-healing infrastructure, and Kubernetes security.
